    Framework Standard

    ISO/IEC 25012 Benchmark Dimensions

    DataVerity measures data quality against international software quality criteria.

    1. Completeness

    Degree to which subject data has values for all expected attributes without silent nulls.

    2. Uniqueness

    Ensures records lack redundant duplicates that cause overcounting in business metrics.

    3. Syntactic Validity

    Conformity of values to schemas, domain rules, ISO dates, and valid regex expressions.

    4. Consistency

    Absence of statistical divergence and anomalous outlier spikes beyond 1.5 × IQR.
